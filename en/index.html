About Us

Why dentists
choose
Sartoretto Verna

“Since 1965, three generations of architects have been turning healthcare spaces into environments that build trust, well-being and growth. Today we bring that experience to your dental practice.”

A patient judges your practice within the first 8 seconds. The waiting room, the reception, the materials, the light: everything communicates before the appointment even begins. With over 2,500 projects in 48 countries and 60 years of experience, we know how to turn your practice into a tool for professional growth.

We do not supply clinical equipment or dental chairs: we take care of what makes your practice unique and recognizable — space design, custom furniture, brand identity and the experience patients have from the very first moment they walk through the door.

Services

What we do
for your practice

We don’t sell catalog furniture. We design your patient’s complete experience, from the outside facade to the last chair in the waiting room.

01

Concept & Strategy

We study your positioning, the kind of patients you want to attract and your local area. We define a concept that sets your practice apart from local competition.

02

Interior Design

We design every space — reception, treatment rooms, waiting areas, kids’ corner — with layouts that optimize patient flow and 3D renderings so you see the result before work begins.

03

Custom Furniture

Furniture designed for your practice, not adapted from a catalog. Certified materials, tailor-made finishes and production in our own workshops with full quality control.

04

Brand Identity

Logo, signage, environmental graphics, coordinated communication. Your practice becomes a recognizable brand that patients remember and recommend.

05

Lighting Design

The right light reduces patient anxiety and improves your work. We design lighting scenarios calibrated for every area of the practice.

06

Turnkey Delivery

A single point of contact from the first consultation to the handover of the keys. We coordinate every phase to deliver your practice ready to use, with no stress and no surprises.
